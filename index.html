What we do

Testing built for the realities of data migration

A migration is only finished when you can demonstrate the new system tells the same story as the old one. We help you plan that proof, build it, and run it.

Data parity validation

Row counts, checksums, aggregates, and column-level comparisons between source and target, down to the record when needed.

Test automation development

Repeatable, version-controlled test frameworks that run on every load, so parity is checked continuously, not once.

Schema & transformation testing

Verify type mappings, business rules, and ETL logic produce the right result, including edge cases, nulls, and encodings.

QA strategy & consultation

Test planning, risk assessment, and sign-off criteria, so stakeholders know exactly what “done” means before cutover.

Performance at scale

Comparison techniques that stay fast on very large tables: partitioned, sampled, or hashed, depending on what the data demands.

Reporting & audit evidence

Clear, defensible reports of what was tested and what matched, ready for compliance, audit, and executive review.

Our approach

From first assessment to final sign-off

  1. 01

    Assess

    Map the source and target ecosystems, data volumes, and the migration plan. Identify where risk concentrates.

  2. 02

    Design

    Define the validation strategy: which checks, at what granularity, with what tolerances and sign-off criteria.

  3. 03

    Build

    Develop automated comparison and test suites tailored to your platforms and integrated with your pipelines.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Run the suites against each migration wave, investigate every discrepancy, and trace it to root cause.

  5. 05

    Certify

    Deliver evidence of parity and a clean handover, with tests your team can keep running after cutover.
